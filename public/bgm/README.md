# Background Music

Add your ambient focus music files here:

- `ambient-focus.mp3` (recommended)
- `ambient-focus.ogg` (fallback)

The music should be:
- Silent/low volume (will be set to 0.1 volume)
- Looping friendly
- Ambient/instrumental for focus and engagement
- Around 2-5 minutes long for seamless looping

You can find free ambient music on sites like:
- Free Music Archive
- YouTube Audio Library
- Epidemic Sound (free tier)

The toggle button in the bottom-right corner allows users to mute/unmute the music.
